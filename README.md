Academic Projects
1. National Survey Data Analysis
Analyzed National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES) data
to examine associations between depression, smoking, and physical
activity among young men.
Key work: - Cleaned and analyzed national survey data using SAS. -
Applied stratified sampling methods. - Examined relationships among
depression, cigarette smoking, physical activity, and age.
Skills: SAS, NHANES, survey data analysis, data cleaning,
exploratory analysis
2. Public Health Forecasting
Used weekly mortality data from the European COVID-19 Forecast Hub from
March 2021 through March 2022 to study COVID-19 mortality trends in
France.
Key work: - Collected and organized publicly available mortality
data. - Applied statistical forecasting methods using MATLAB and
RStudio. - Compared modeling approaches during periods of declining
COVID-19 deaths. - Demonstrated how short-term forecasting can support
public health decision-making.
Skills: MATLAB, R, time-series forecasting, public health analytics
3. ReachOut Health Promotion Plan
Developed a health promotion program designed to expand access to mental
health services for high school students in rural Georgia.
Key work: - Designed individual interventions including a
Text-4-Help support line and counseling services. - Developed group
interventions including peer-led outreach, group counseling, household
counseling, and a youth mental health expo. - Planned community-level
resources and engagement initiatives.
Skills: Program development, public health planning, community
outreach, intervention design
4. COVID-19 Case Time Series Forecasting
Analyzed World Health Organization COVID-19 case data across Europe from
2020--2022 and aggregated 156 observations into a univariate time
series.
Key work: - Evaluated baseline persistence, ARIMA, and Holt-Winters
exponential smoothing models. - Used an 80/20 chronological train/test
split. - Evaluated forecasts with MAE and RMSE. - Identified volatility,
non-stationarity, and weekly reporting patterns. - Found that the
baseline persistence model produced the lowest forecast error among the
tested approaches.
Skills: Python, time-series analysis, ARIMA, Holt-Winters
exponential smoothing, MAE, RMSE, model evaluation
5. Cardiovascular Predictive Modeling
Analyzed 2021--2023 NHANES dietary, blood pressure, cholesterol, and
demographic data to investigate relationships between dietary intake and
cardiovascular outcomes.
Key work: - Merged datasets containing 7,801 participants and 208
variables. - Evaluated protein, sugar, fat, and fiber as predictors of
blood pressure and cholesterol. - Built linear regression, polynomial
regression, Ridge, Lasso, and Elastic Net models. - Evaluated model
performance using MSE and R-squared. - Found weak overall predictive
relationships, with polynomial regression producing the strongest
blood-pressure performance among the tested models with an R-squared of
0.0514.
Skills: Python, NHANES, data integration, regression,
regularization, predictive modeling, exploratory data analysis
6. Flight Delay Classification
Used U.S. commercial flight data to build and evaluate a J48
decision-tree classifier in WEKA.
Key work: - Prepared 300 flight records for classification. - Used
scheduled departure time, carrier, origin, destination, and distance as
model inputs. - Evaluated the model with 10-fold cross-validation. -
Achieved 59.33% classification accuracy. - Identified scheduled
departure time as the primary predictor selected by the resulting
decision tree. - Identified weather, airport traffic, aircraft
availability, and seasonal factors as potential variables for future
model improvement.
Skills: WEKA, J48 decision trees, classification, cross-validation,
feature analysis, model evaluation
7. Airline Market Entry Analysis
Analyzed airline traffic and route performance to evaluate flight
delays, revenue, profitability, and potential market-entry
opportunities.
Key work: - Identified 12,595 delayed flights in the analyzed
data. - Examined delay patterns across airport sizes. - Identified
SLC-TWF as the highest-revenue and highest-profit roundtrip route in the
analysis. - Evaluated additional routes for potential future
investment. - Proposed further analysis of empty-seat costs, route
optimization, revenue forecasting, and passenger-volume forecasting.
Skills: Aviation analytics, profitability analysis, route analysis,
data-driven decision support
8. PostgreSQL Transaction Management & Database Operations
Developed a Python application that connects to a PostgreSQL relational
database and performs transaction-controlled SQL operations.
Key work: - Connected Python to PostgreSQL using psycopg2. -
Executed SQL DELETE, INSERT, and SELECT operations. - Managed
related Product, Stock, and Depot records. - Applied explicit
transaction isolation and commit/rollback handling. - Implemented
exception handling and database connection cleanup. - Displayed query
results in tabular form using tabulate.
Skills: Python, SQL, PostgreSQL, psycopg2, relational databases,
transaction management, exception handling, data integrity
